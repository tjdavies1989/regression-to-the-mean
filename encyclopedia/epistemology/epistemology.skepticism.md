# Skepticism

Nothing in your experience distinguishes it from the experience of a brain in a vat, fed exactly the sensory stream you are having now. The observation becomes an argument with one further, almost invisible premise. You do not know you are not such a brain; if you know you have hands, then — since having hands entails not being a handless brain — you know you are not one; so you do not know you have hands. The second premise is an instance of *closure*: know something, competently deduce a consequence, and you know the consequence. Deny it and deduction stops transmitting knowledge; accept it and the vat swallows everything resting on perception. The conclusion is unbelievable; the premises are individually hard to refuse. Since its descent this node divides the study of which to refuse anyway among five studies — four responses to the Cartesian argument, and the older challenge that none of them touches.

**Closure and its deniers** (`closure`) is where the argument's engine is examined. Formulated carefully (Hawthorne, Williamson), closure is as secure as any epistemic principle; Dretske's zebras and the relevant-alternatives idea, and Nozick's sensitivity condition — you would not believe it if it were false — buy immunity from the vat by letting your hand-belief track truth while your anti-vat belief cannot. The price is DeRose's abominable conjunctions: "I know I have hands, but I do not know I am not a handless brain in a vat." Safety, sensitivity's modal cousin, preserves closure and pays elsewhere; whether denying closure is a cure worse than the disease is the child's live question.

**Moorean responses** (`moorean`) run the argument backwards — one philosopher's modus ponens is another's modus tollens — and the child's work is to say what entitles the reversal. Moore's proof of an external world stands or falls on whether warrant transmits across it, which Wright denies (transmission failure, and an entitlement to the hinge instead); Pryor's dogmatism supplies the machinery the Moorean needs — perceptual experience confers immediate, defeasible justification not hostage to first ruling out skeptical alternatives — and meets its own objection from cognitive penetration, adjudicated jointly with `mind.perception`; neo-Moorean externalists (Sosa, Williamson) locate the entitlement in the reliability or the knowledge itself. Contextualism, the other great closure-keeping response, is homed at `language.context` and `epistemology.knowledge` and only summoned here: "know" shifts standards with the conversation, so skepticism is true only in the seminar that voices it.

**Semantic responses** (`semantic`) attack the first premise from the theory of reference: Putnam argued that a lifelong envatted brain's word "vat" would refer to vat-images, not vats, so its sentence "I am a brain in a vat" would be false — the hypothesis cannot be truly thought by its own victim. The child treats the argument's logic (Brueckner), the recent-envatment loophole, and the standing verdict that it refutes one formulation of skepticism rather than skepticism.

**Hinge epistemology** (`hinge`) denies that "I have hands" is an ordinary knowledge claim at all. Wittgenstein's *On Certainty*, provoked by Moore, treats such certainties as hinges — the fixed points on which doubting and checking turn, neither known nor doubted — and the contemporary readings divide over what standing that is: Moyal-Sharrock's framework reading, Wright's entitlements, Pritchard's non-belief account, Coliva's constitutivism. The child also carries the extension to religious hinges and the question whether hinges survive closure.

**Pyrrhonism** (`pyrrhonism`) is the older and arguably deeper challenge. The Agrippan trilemma — every justification ends in regress, circle, or brute assumption — targets not perception but reasoning itself, and no closure-denying or context-shifting maneuver reaches it. Sextus's Pyrrhonism was a practice rather than a thesis: oppose equally strong arguments until judgment suspends itself, and find tranquility following. The apraxia objection — that suspension cannot be lived — and the appearances reply, the rustic and urbane readings (Barnes, Burnyeat, Frede), Fogelin's neo-Pyrrhonism, and the careful comparison with Indian and Buddhist suspensions of judgment are the child's terrain, and its setting is `traditions.hellenistic`.

Behind all five stands a question the division's root raised: whether the response to skepticism is an answer at all, or a diagnosis of why the argument compels in the seminar and evaporates at the airport — and whether that evaporation is a refutation or a symptom.

## Children

- `closure` — **Closure and Its Deniers** — whether knowledge transmits across known entailment; relevant alternatives, Nozick's tracking, and the abominable conjunctions. *(standard)*
- `moorean` — **Moorean Responses and Dogmatism** — Moore's proof of an external world, Pryor's dogmatism, and what entitles reversing the skeptic's argument. *(standard)*
- `semantic` — **Semantic Responses** — Putnam's argument that "I am a brain in a vat" is self-refuting, and its limits. *(satellite)*
- `hinge` — **Hinge Epistemology** — *On Certainty* and its descendants: commitments that are neither known nor doubted, and the entitlement theories built on them. *(standard)*
- `pyrrhonism` — **Pyrrhonian Skepticism** — the Agrippan modes, suspension of judgment as a way of life, and the apraxia objection. *(anchor)*
