# Metaphilosophy

Philosophy is the only discipline for which "what is this discipline?" is an internal research question. Physics does not await a physics of physics; philosophy cannot avoid a philosophy of philosophy, because its self-conception settles method, and method settles results. Is philosophy continuous with science, as naturalists hold — same evidence, wider questions? Is it conceptual analysis, mapping what we mean rather than what there is? Is it phenomenological description, therapy for linguistic bewitchment, the construction of better concepts than the ones we inherited, or the articulation of how things in the widest sense hang together? Each answer has held the field somewhere, and each makes different work look like progress.

Two live debates organize the register. First, **method**: what are philosophy's instruments, and why should they be trusted? Intuitions elicited by thought experiments have carried enormous weight — Gettier cases, trolley cases, Twin Earth — and the experimental philosophy movement's finding that such intuitions vary across populations put the practice itself on trial. Second, **progress and disagreement**: philosophy's problems are old and its experts disagree about nearly all of them; whether this shows the field defective, uniquely hard, or misdescribed (its progress lying in distinctions, refutations, and the disciplines it has repeatedly spun off) is itself contested.

This register is deliberately small — class `standard`, per the constitution's own advice that self-reference is a place where depth is cheap and discipline is dear.

## Children

- `what-is-philosophy` — **What Is Philosophy?** — the rival self-conceptions and what hangs on the choice. *(standard)*
- `methods` — **Philosophical Methods** — analysis, thought experiments, formal methods, phenomenology, genealogy, experimental philosophy. *(standard)*
- `progress` — **Progress in Philosophy** — whether there is any, and what would count. *(standard)*
- `disagreement` — **Peer Disagreement in Philosophy** — what persistent expert disagreement does to philosophical belief. *(satellite)*

## Bridges

Method questions bridge to `epistemology` (intuitions as evidence, the a priori) and `logic` (formal methods); the progress question bridges to `science` (theory change) and to the `traditions` register, whose plurality is the strongest datum against any single self-conception.
